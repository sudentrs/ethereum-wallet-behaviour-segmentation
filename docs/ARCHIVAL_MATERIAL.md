# Historical Material Policy

This repository preserves earlier work so that the project history remains
auditable. The final manuscript does **not** use every tracked result.

## Current Submission Evidence

Use only the year-end code-free count/activity/direction study:

- `overleaf/main.tex` and `overleaf/supplementary.tex`
- `results_v3/year_end_2022_full_cohort/`
- `results_v3/year_end_2022_count_cohort/`
- `docs/CURRENT_MANUSCRIPT_STATUS.md`
- `docs/REFEREE_REVISION.md`
- `docs/REPRODUCE_CURRENT_VALIDATION.md`

## Historical, Non-Citable Paths

- `results/`: earlier experiments, including pre-controlled cohort work.
- `results_v2/`: earlier EOA-only, K4/K5/K6, and review-stage outputs.
- `docs/REVIEW_REVISION_STATUS.md`, `docs/PIPELINE.md`,
  `docs/PROJECT_GUIDE.md`, and `docs/SCRIPT_INDEX.md`: useful implementation
  history, but not the final paper protocol.
- `docs/TCSS_REFEREE_AUDIT.md` and `docs/VALIDATION_COMPLETION.md`: earlier
  audits retained for traceability. Their headers identify where later controls
  supersede them.

Do not delete these paths when reproducing a historical result. Do not use them
to replace the cohort, outcomes, figures, or claims in the submitted paper.
